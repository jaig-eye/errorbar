# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want errorbar to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and propose
changes without being treated badly. Everyone taking part is expected to be respectful and
constructive, whatever their background or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give feedback that helps: specific, about the work, and with a way forward.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and credit the people whose work you build on.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, and unwelcome attention.
- Anything else that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions that do
not follow this code, and may block people who break it. To report a problem, use GitHub's
"Report content" on the comment, or write to the maintainer through their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/jaig-eye). Reports are handled in confidence.
